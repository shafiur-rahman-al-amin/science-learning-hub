Personal Introduction

Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem.

Assalamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullahi wa Barakatuh.

My name is Mohammad Safiur Rahman Al Amin. I have completed my Alim examination from the prestigious Darunnazat Siddikia Kamil Madrasah.

I am interested in exploring the relationship between Quranic teachings, knowledge, modern scientific discoveries, personal development, and the signs of Allah throughout creation.

Quranic Wisdom

Guidance for the Mind and the Soul

Quranic verses on knowledge, self-transformation, righteousness, provision, and reflection upon the universe.

01 · Knowledge and Excellence
يَرْفَعِ اللَّهُ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا مِنكُمْ وَالَّذِينَ أُوتُوا الْعِلْمَ دَرَجَاتٍ

“Allah will raise those who have believed among you and those who were given knowledge, by degrees.”

Surah Al-Mujadila · 58:11
02 · Change and Self-Development
إِنَّ اللَّهَ لَا يُغَيِّرُ مَا بِقَوْمٍ حَتَّىٰ يُغَيِّرُوا مَا بِأَنفُسِهِمْ

“Indeed, Allah will not change the condition of a people until they change what is within themselves.”

Surah Ar-Ra’d · 13:11
03 · Taqwa and Unexpected Provision
وَمَن يَتَّقِ اللَّهَ يَجْعَل لَّهُ مَخْرَجًا ۝ وَيَرْزُقْهُ مِنْ حَيْثُ لَا يَحْتَسِبُ

“And whoever fears Allah, He will make for him a way out and provide for him from where he does not expect.”

Surah At-Talaq · 65:2–3

Faith and Reflection

The Quran and Modern Science

Reflecting upon the natural world through the lens of revelation, observation, and the pursuit of knowledge.

The Signs of Allah in Creation

The Quran repeatedly invites humanity to reflect upon the heavens, the earth, and the human self. These invitations encourage observation, contemplation, and the pursuit of beneficial knowledge.

وَهُوَ الَّذِي أَنشَأَ لَكُمُ السَّمْعَ وَالْأَبْصَارَ وَالْأَفْئِدَةَ ۚ قَلِيلًا مَّا تَشْكُرُونَ

“And it is He who produced for you hearing and vision and hearts; little are you grateful.”

Surah Al-Mu’minun · 23:78

Human Development and Reflection

ثُمَّ خَلَقْنَا النُّطْفَةَ عَلَقَةً فَخَلَقْنَا الْعَلَقَةَ مُضْغَةً فَخَلَقْنَا الْمُضْغَةَ عِظَامًا فَكَسَوْنَا الْعِظَامَ لَحْمًا

“Then We made the drop into a clinging substance, and We made the clinging substance into a lump, and We made the lump into bones, and We covered the bones with flesh.”

Excerpt from Surah Al-Mu’minun · 23:14

These passages encourage reflection on life, human development, and the natural world. Scientific inquiry requires careful observation, reliable evidence, and intellectual honesty. Understanding the relationship between scripture and scientific findings calls for thoughtful interpretation rather than unsupported claims.

Urdu Poetry and Reflection

Urdu verses and poetic expressions inspired by themes of truth, love, self-awareness, and devotion.

تُو شَمَعِ حقِیقَت ہے تو پروانہ بن جاعِشقِ الٰہی میں فنا ہو کر دیوانہ بن جا

“If you stand before the lamp of truth, become the moth drawn toward its light. Surrender yourself to divine love and become a devoted lover of truth.”

جسم کو دیکھ کر روح کا اندازہ نہ کرروح تو وہ سمندر ہے جس کا کوئی کنارہ نہیں

“Do not measure the depth of a soul by looking at the outward body. The soul is like an ocean whose shores cannot be found.”

A poetic expression supplied in the spirit of Rumi’s themes; original authorship has not been independently verified.

“The purpose of worship is not merely the bowing of the body; it is the surrender of the heart before the Lord.”

My Vision and Purpose

My Aspiration to Become an Islamic Da’i

My long-term aspiration is to become a sincere, knowledgeable, and thoughtful Islamic Da’i who can convey the teachings of the Quran and Sunnah with wisdom, compassion, and clarity.

I hope to strengthen my understanding of Aqidah, authentic Islamic knowledge, and contemporary issues so that I can communicate beneficial teachings in ways that people can understand and apply in their lives.

I aspire to pursue further studies at the Islamic University of Madinah, Saudi Arabia, if Allah grants me the opportunity. I pray that my learning remains sincere and becomes a means of benefit to others.

May Allah grant me sincerity, beneficial knowledge, righteous character, and the ability to serve His religion in accordance with the Quran and authentic Sunnah.

Ameen, Ya Rabbal Aalameen. Insha’Allah.
